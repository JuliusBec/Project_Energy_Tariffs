DYNERGY – Introduction and Context
==================================
 
Introduction
------------
 
DYNERGY is a web application for analysing and comparing fixed and dynamic electricity tariffs based on real consumption data.
The project was carried out at the Chair of Information Systems and Business Analytics and received technical support from Nico, Robin and Tim.
The goal of the application is to provide users with a transparent, data-driven basis for making informed tariff decisions.
 
Motivation
----------
 
From 2025 onwards, energy suppliers are legally required to offer dynamic electricity tariffs in addition to traditional fixed-price tariffs.
Dynamic tariffs are linked to the hourly changing prices on the electricity market, which makes their cost structure difficult to assess for many households.
In particular, during periods of strong price fluctuations, it is unclear whether a dynamic tariff is actually cheaper or not.
DYNERGY addresses this uncertainty by analysing consumption data and automatically calculating tariff costs.
 
Project Canvas
--------------
 
At the beginning of the development process, a project canvas was created to structure the planning of the project.
The canvas defined, among others, the following elements:
 
- environment and relevant framework conditions
- key milestones
- functional scope of the application
- basic requirements for usability and user value
 
The canvas specified that DYNERGY should be a web app capable of processing real consumption data, mapping different tariff models and presenting results in an understandable way.
In addition, requirements such as intuitive usability and the potential for cost and energy savings were formulated.
The canvas served as a guiding framework for all subsequent development steps.
 
Problem Statement
-----------------
Electricity tariffs differ significantly in their structure, and many users struggle to assess which tariff will be cheaper for them in the long run.
Fixed-price tariffs offer stable prices, whereas dynamic tariffs reflect fluctuations on the electricity market.
Without analysing their own consumption profile, users often make subjective or poorly informed tariff choices.
DYNERGY tackles this issue by enabling an objective evaluation of actual consumption against different tariff models.
 
Project Objectives and Functional Scope
---------------------------------------
 
DYNERGY supports users both with and without smart meters.
For this purpose, two data sources are available:
 
- annual consumption (manual input)
- CSV upload with 15-minute consumption data
 
Based on these data, the system automatically calculates the costs for various fixed and dynamic electricity tariffs.
The results are visualised in a structured way so that it becomes immediately clear which tariff is most cost-effective for the individual consumption profile.
The application thus replaces rough estimates with an objective, data-driven decision basis.
